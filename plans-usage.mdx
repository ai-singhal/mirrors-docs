Mirrors meters work in **replay-minutes**: the minutes an environment's container spends running, which is the time your agent can call its tools. Building and exploring environments is free; the meter runs while a container is up, and it scales to zero on its own once nothing has called it for a while.

## How much does Mirrors cost?

Mirrors has three plans. **Free** costs \$0/month, includes 60 replay-minutes
each month, and charges \$0.20 per replay-minute after that. **Startup** is
\$499/month, includes 2,000 replay-minutes, and drops the metered rate to
\$0.15. **Enterprise** is custom volume pricing. There is no seat licence and no
minimum commitment on any of them.

| Plan | Price | Included | Overage | What you get |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | \$0/month | 60 replay-minutes/month | \$0.20 per replay-minute | Unlimited environments, public `/v1` API. A payment method is saved at signup, but \$0 is due today. |
| **Startup** | \$499/month | 2,000 replay-minutes/month | \$0.15 per replay-minute | Everything in Free, at a lower metered rate, plus priority support. Cancel anytime. |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Volume pricing | Negotiated | SSO and role controls, security review, on-premises or BYOC options. [Contact us](https://www.runmirrors.com/contact). |

See the current public prices at [runmirrors.com/pricing](https://www.runmirrors.com/pricing). Your plan, included usage, and metered usage are visible in the dashboard under **Billing**.

## Where can I see how many replay-minutes I have used?

In three places, all showing the same numbers: `mirrors usage` in the CLI, `GET
/v1/usage` over the API, and the **Billing** section of the dashboard.

```bash
mirrors usage        # CLI: replay-minutes used vs. included allowance
```

```bash
curl https://api.runmirrors.com/v1/usage \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer mk_live_..."
```

The dashboard sidebar also shows a near-limit meter as you approach your monthly allowance.

## What happens when I use up my included minutes?

Nothing breaks and nothing is deleted. On Free, usage past the included 60
replay-minutes is billed at \$0.20 per minute on your saved card. Included
minutes reset at the start of each month, and your environments, traces, and
history remain available throughout.

<Tip>
  Rate limits are separate from the plan allowance: `/v1` requests are also rate-limited per workspace and return `429` when exceeded. See the [API introduction](/api-reference/introduction).
</Tip>
